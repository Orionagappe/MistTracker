**Consequence**: This explains why the emotional domain is foundational, not peripheral. Remove it from a 13-validator system and you get mechanical consensus that falls apart under sustained pressure.

**13-Validator Council**:

**17-Validator Council**:
- Total: 17 validators
- 2/3 threshold: 12 validators (for binding decision)

- Margin: 2 validators (more resilient)

**Implication**:

**Consequence**: For domain-level decisions (atomic physics validation, psionics claims), 13 validators suffice. For global decisions (framework changes, validator certification standards), you need 17.
