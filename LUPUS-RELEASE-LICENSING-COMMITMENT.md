## Open Questions Resolved

### Q: Why GPL v2 instead of MIT?
**A**: GPL v2 ensures that improvements to medical simulations stay open and available to the research community. Copyleft prevents commercial enclosure of validation work.

### Q: Can I use this in commercial research?
**A**: Yes. You can license and sell services based on the simulation. You must provide source code to your customers and credit the original work.

### Q: What if trials fail?
**A**: If validation does NOT succeed, the code remains proprietary until future validation occurs. GPL v2 release is tied to successful clinical evidence.

### Q: Can I change the license?
**A**: No. GPL v2 is permanent. Any derived work must also be GPL v2. This protects the openness of medical research.

### Q: What about patent claims?
**A**: GPL v2 includes patent rights clauses. Anyone using the code grants downstream users patent rights related to the code.
