## Tracking Tips

- **Be honest**: Record actual numbers, not target numbers
- **Identify triggers**: Note what leads to smoking (stress, breaks, social, etc.)
- **Pattern recognition**: Look for high-smoke times/days
- **Replacement plan**: What will you do instead? (gum, walks, water, etc.)
- **Celebrate wins**: Note when you skip or reduce

## Reduction Strategy

### Weeks 1-2: Establish baseline
- Track without changing much
- Identify your natural patterns
- Plan replacement activities

### Weeks 3-4: Small reductions
- Reduce by 10-20% from baseline
- Focus on easiest-to-skip situations
- Build alternative habits

### Weeks 5-8: Progressive tapering
- Continue 10% monthly reduction
- Replace cigarettes with alternatives
- Build momentum

**Note**: Once patches arrive, this timeline may accelerate—follow the patch schedule and your body's signals.

## Alternatives to Try

When you get the urge:
- ☐ Drink water or herbal tea
- ☐ Take a 5-minute walk
- ☐ Chew gum or mints
- ☐ Do deep breathing (box breathing: 4-4-4-4)
- ☐ Call/text a friend
- ☐ Stretch or light exercise
- ☐ Journal/write thoughts
- ☐ Change environment
