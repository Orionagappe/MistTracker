## Overview

This document explores the integration of antimony as a "single atom fuse" within quantum semiconductor research. The concept leverages antimony's unique properties to serve as an atomic-scale threshold, trigger, or safeguard in advanced quantum devices and circuits.

## Antimony: Single Atom Fuse Principle

- **Definition:** Antimony is proposed as a single-atom fuse—a critical, non-reversible trigger or safety mechanism at the atomic scale.
- **Application:** In quantum semiconductors, a single antimony atom can act as a fuse, initiating a cascade or protecting system integrity when a threshold is crossed (e.g., overcurrent, quantum state collapse, or error event).
- **Symbolic Value:** Represents atomic-scale precision, irreversible triggers, and last-resort safety mechanisms in device design.

4. **System-Level Implications:**
