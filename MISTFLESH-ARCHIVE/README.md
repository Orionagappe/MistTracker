# Mystery Flesh Pit National Park — Archive Collection

*A collection of artifacts & ephemera relating to the now-defunct Mystery Flesh Pit. Written & Illustrated by Trevor Roberts.*

## Overview

This repository contains a curated archive of documentation, field observations, maintenance records, and visitor materials from **Mystery Flesh Pit National Park**, a protected anomalous site that operated between 2003 and 2007 before being sealed indefinitely.

The materials here represent authentic artifacts recovered from the archive and made available under current licensing terms. They document the final operational period of the park through a variety of institutional voices: park rangers, maintenance staff, regulatory compliance officers, research teams, and visitors.

## What This Archive Contains

- **Incident Reports** — Official accounts of convergence events and anomalous phenomena
- **Visitor Logs** — Personal observations and visitor experience records
- **Maintenance Records** — Technical documentation of site infrastructure and integrity monitoring
- **Ephemera** — Miscellaneous materials (photographs, diagrams, artifact catalogs, field notes)

## Browsing the Collection

Start with [MISTFLESH-ARCHIVE-MANIFEST.md](MISTFLESH-ARCHIVE-MANIFEST.md) for a complete inventory and chronological overview.

Each section is organized by document type and date. Materials are presented as-is, with minimal editorial intervention.

## On the "Golden Apple"

Researchers and visitors noted recurring references to an inexplicable phenomenon designated informally as "the golden apple." Its exact nature remains undocumented in official records. Multiple incident reports reference it; its appearances seem correlated with periods of heightened site activity. See ephemera/ for related observations.

## Technical Note on Integrity

All records have been verified for authenticity through available cryptographic metadata and cross-referenced against archived audit trails. Hash chain continuity checks are documented in maintenance-records/.
