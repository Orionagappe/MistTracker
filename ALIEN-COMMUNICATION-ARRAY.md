## The Array Structure

### Layer 1: Detection — Voyager Reference Frame

**What**: Live Voyager data streams + gravitational wave arrays (LIGO, future space-based detectors)

**Purpose**: Listen for disturbances in space-time that indicate presence of other intelligences

**Capability**: When something unusual happens (gravity wave, anomalous trajectory shift, unexpected signal), the system flags it immediately

**Message it sends**: "We are paying attention. We notice cosmic events. We validate our understanding against reality."

### Layer 2: Interpretation — Validator Network

**What**: Enigma specialists trained to handle unprecedented phenomena

**Purpose**: When something truly alien is detected, validators work through it together (peer lattice, not hierarchy)

**Capability**: Can navigate genuine uncertainty, hold paradox, resist both skeptic dismissal and believer fraud

**Message it sends**: "We can handle what we don't understand. We won't panic or collapse into false certainty."

### Layer 3: Documentation — Blockchain Audit Chain

**What**: Every detection, every interpretation, every verdict recorded immutably

**Purpose**: Transparency. Anyone analyzing our framework sees our exact thought process.

**Capability**: Full accountability. No hidden decisions. "Here's how we think. Here's what we got wrong. Here's what we learned."

**Message it sends**: "We are honest about our reasoning. You can trust our verdicts because you can see how we reached them."

### Layer 4: Verification — Cosmic Perspective Validation

**What**: Voyager coherence scoring from 20B km away

**Purpose**: Demonstrate that our framework maintains integrity even when observed from cosmic distance

**Capability**: External verification that cannot be faked. Distance and delay prove authenticity.

**Message it sends**: "We hold ourselves to standards that transcend our own perspective. We validate ourselves against witnesses we can't control."

### Layer 5: Principles — Non-Interference

**What**: Framework explicitly prohibits control, demands respect for autonomy

**Purpose**: Show that we won't try to dominate visitors; we respect their independence

**Capability**: Institutional commitment (built into validator training, peer lattice structure, moderator rights) to guidance over control

**Message it sends**: "We have learned that cooperation requires respecting the other's autonomy. We won't try to force you into our frameworks."
