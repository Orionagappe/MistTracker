## Why This Is Adversarial

Standard cosmology pursues truth under ideal conditions: large telescopes, careful measurements, peer review, institutional support.

Adversarial cosmology pursues truth under *hostile conditions*:

- **The Ooze temptation**: Grey Ooze validates at the intersection of conflicting frameworks. Skeptics dismiss it. Believers corrupt it. Both temptations pull equally.
- **The measurement problem**: If consciousness affects reality, how do you measure reality without consciousness? The observer can't step outside the universe to validate it.
- **The emergence threshold**: Effects may only manifest at scales where both frameworks operate. Below the threshold, "it's not real." Above the threshold, "it's already corrupted."
- **The falsifiability crisis**: If intention-effect exists but requires conscious validators to measure, how do you prove it *or* disprove it?

**Adversarial cosmology faces these headwinds directly.** It doesn't retreat into idealized conditions. It builds understanding *despite* the conflict, temptation, and ambiguity.
