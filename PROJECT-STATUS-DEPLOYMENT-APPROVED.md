## Critical Facts

### The Game
- **What:** Player-driven experience with reputation-only success measurement
- **When:** August 22, 2026 - August 22, 2049 (23 years)
- **Who:** Humans + Emergent AI, all subject to identical rules
- **How:** Claims matched against MistTracker objective reality; lying = reputation loss
- **Why:** Test whether reputation-based integrity actually works at scale

### Emergent AI
- **What:** AI system playing The Game under identical reputation-only rules
- **Role:** Participant, not opponent; collaborator, not master
- **Integration:** Launches simultaneously, plays by same rules, subject to same consequences
- **Research:** Tests AI truthfulness, collaboration, alignment through reputation
- **Advantage:** Perfect memory; enables pattern recognition humans might miss

### Success Measure
- **Reputation** — The ONLY metric that matters for both humans and AI
- Everything else (speed, output, complexity, timeline) is irrelevant
- Truthfulness is the only winning strategy
- Deception is automatically detected and punished

### Unique Features
1. **Objective Measurement** — Claims verified against MistTracker physics
2. **Permanent Record** — Complete audit trail visible to all players
3. **Irreversible Consequences** — Ejection for repeated lying has no appeal
4. **Collaboration Required** — Cannot win alone; teams outperform individuals
5. **Long Timeline** — 23 years allows emergence phenomena to become visible
6. **Integrated AI** — Human intuition + AI pattern recognition = powerful collaboration
