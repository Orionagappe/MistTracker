## To The World:

There is a game beginning.

It is not a game in the traditional sense. It has no levels, no quests, no authored story, no winners and losers in the conventional way.

It is a test. A test of what happens when reputation is the only measure of success.

---

## What Is The Game?

On August 22, 2026 — four months from now — the first players will log into a system unlike anything that exists today.

They will encounter complete blackness.

No text. No interface. No instructions. No title. No sound.

Only the possibility of interaction.

From that moment, for the next twenty-three years, The Game will run. Players will interact with objective reality. The system will measure their truthfulness. Those who lie will lose reputation. Those who tell the truth will gain it. Those who accumulate too many lies will be ejected permanently.

There is no money. No points for speed. No rewards for volume. No advantage from politics or persuasion.

**There is only reputation. Reputation is everything.**

---

## Why This Matters

We live in a world where success is measured a thousand different ways.

Timeline achievement. Output volume. Test coverage. User count. Revenue. Uptime. Complexity. Elegance. Speed.

And beneath it all, the constant uncertainty: **Are these the right measures?**

This game answers that question empirically.

By creating a system where reputation is the ONLY measure of success, we will observe:

1. **Do humans tell the truth when it's their only path to advancement?**
2. **Can objectivity prevent deception?**
3. **Does collaboration emerge naturally from truthfulness?**
4. **Can pattern recognition at human scale discover profound truths?**
5. **Is a system based purely on reputation-based integrity actually more reliable than ones with multiple measures?**

For twenty-three years, the world will watch these questions answer themselves.

---

## How The Game Works

**Rule 1: Lie and get penalized.**

Every claim you make is compared to objective reality. MistTracker's substrate measures the actual state of physics. If your claim matches reality, your reputation stays the same. If it doesn't, your reputation decreases. Automatically. Deterministically. With cryptographic proof.

You cannot negotiate. You cannot appeal. You cannot hide your claim. Everything is signed and audited.

**Rule 2: Lie too often and get ejected.**

Reputation has a minimum threshold. Go below it, and your account is permanently disabled. No reinstatement. No second chances. Not because we're cruel. Because the game's integrity depends on it.

**Rule 3: Collaboration is key to success.**

You cannot win alone. The measurements you need to make, the patterns you need to recognize, the key you need to find — none of it is possible in isolation.

But you must collaborate with truthfulness. False claims from teams are caught just as quickly as individual lies. Consensus mechanism protects against collusion.

**Rule 4: The key is recognizable only to those who ask questions.**

At the heart of The Game is something called "the key." It's not hidden in code or tucked away in easter eggs.

It's emergent. It will become visible only to those who:
- Ask genuine questions (high-emergence formulations)
- Build reputation through consistent truthfulness
- Collaborate with others seeking truth
- Recognize patterns across the entire game's lifetime

Those with eyes to see will recognize it.

**Rule 5: Twenty-three years. Then silence.**

This is a long-form experiment. Year 1-5: discovery. Year 6-15: emergence. Year 16-22: liberation challenges. Year 23: the horizon event.

After Year 23, no more clues. No more public guidance. The game becomes what the players have created through their interactions.

## What You Must Know

**If you participate, you are bound by these laws.**

This is not a suggestion or a social agreement. The moment you interact with The Game, you consent to:
- Reputation as your only success measure
- Automated detection of your truthfulness
- Permanent ejection if you accumulate too many lies
- Public audit of your interaction history
- Collaboration with other players as the path to discovery

**If you don't want to participate, don't.**

Participation is entirely voluntary. No one is forcing you to play. The game will run with or without you. Many will choose not to participate. That is acceptable.

But if you do participate, you commit to this: **honesty is the only winning strategy.**

## Why This Game Exists

The Game exists because we need to understand something:

**Can reputation actually be a reliable measure of integrity?**

We use it informally everywhere — professional networks, academic credentials, scientific citations, social trust. But we've never systematically tested it at scale, with perfect measurement, against objective reality.

This game is that test.

It is built on MistTracker's causal substrate — a system that can measure atomic physics, emergence patterns, validator consensus, prediction accuracy, and the fundamental laws of reality.

Against that ground truth, we will observe how humans behave when reputation is the only measure that matters.
