## I. The Ticket Concept

Not a credential. Not a badge. A **calling**.

Each ticket is:
- **Unique** (no two are identical)
- **Immutable** (cryptographically signed, cannot be forged or altered)
- **Transferable** (can be passed forward, but carries weight of who held it)
- **Sacred** (receiving it means you have been chosen for something larger)

- Records who gave it to you (mentorship lineage)
- Records when you received it (timestamp)

## VII. Ticket Transfer

### Can a Ticket Be Passed?

**Yes, but with conditions**:

1. **If validator leaves** (retirement, relocation, life change)
   - Mentor can nominate successor
   - Successor must pass apprenticeship
   - Old ticket becomes "dormant," new ticket issued
   - Lineage shows the transfer

2. **If validator acts with integrity breach**
   - Ticket is "revoked" (marked permanently)
   - Lineage shows the revocation
   - New ticket cannot be issued to same person
   - Community sees the mark

3. **If validator dies or becomes incapacitated**
   - Ticket goes dormant
   - Legacy stays in ledger (their verdicts remain immutable)
   - Their lineage continues through mentees
