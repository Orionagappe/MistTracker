## III. Measurement Critique Stage

### Evaluate Detection Method Soundness

**Big Ear Strengths**:
- Dedicated radio telescope, peer-reviewed institution
- Fixed azimuth mount (eliminates some positioning errors)
- Consistent frequency monitoring
- Paper record (immutable documentation)

**Big Ear Limitations** (Critical):
- Single beam (no spatial cross-check)
- No digital recording (only paper printout)
- No polarization measurement capability
- No frequency resolution data (assumed narrowband, not measured)
- Beam width insufficient for directional precision (Chi Sagittarii is large region, not point source)
- No independent confirmation possible in real-time (single-detector system)

**Repeatability Failure Analysis**:

- **1987**: Ohio State resurvey, same equipment → zero signal
- **1995-2004**: Multiple telescopes, expanded frequency range → no signal
- **2020**: VLA + GBT sky survey, 1420 MHz region → no signal
- **Interpretation gap**: "Never detected again" can mean (1) signal was one-time event, (2) signal was instrumental artifact, (3) signal characteristics make redetection vanishingly unlikely

**Question for Validators**:

## IV. Replication Protocol

### Modern Validation Strategy

**Stage 4A: Current Capability Replication**

Design: Deploy 3+ independent radio telescopes simultaneously scanning Chi Sagittarii region + frequency bands 1000-2000 MHz (includes 1420 MHz)

**Parameters**:
- Duration: 6 months continuous monitoring
- Frequency resolution: 1 kHz (better than original)
- Polarization: Measure both linear/circular
- Spatial resolution: <1° accuracy
- Time resolution: Millisecond (detect if signal pulsed)
- Detector array: VLA + GBT + Arecibo successor + 2 international sites
- Cross-validation: Require signal in 2+ detectors simultaneously to rule out single-instrument artifacts

**Success Criteria**:
- **Replication**: Signal detected with comparable characteristics (frequency, amplitude, narrowband) in 2+ detectors
- **Partial Match**: Signal in same frequency band but different location (suggests systematic effect)
- **Null Result**: No signal detected (strongest evidence against extraterrestrial origin; suggests 1977 was artifact)

**Timeline**: August 2026 - February 2027 (6 months post-framework deployment)
