# Game Breadcrumbs: Narrative Style Guide

**Purpose**: Clues woven into world dialogue and flavor text that spark curiosity and lead to validator system discovery passively.

**Principle**: Never explain the system. Always make players curious about what they overheard.

---

## NPC Dialogue (Spark Conversation)

### Market Vendor
> "Yeah, my last shipment got flagged. Someone had to validate it before the traders would touch it. Three days of waiting."

**What player does:** Asks "flagged by who?" → learns about validators

### Scholar in Library
> "I'm writing this up carefully. Last researcher who submitted sloppily got reputation damage. Won't recover for months."

**What player does:** Asks "reputation with who?" → discovers validator pathway

### Tavern Gossip
> "They say the new judges don't just vote anymore. They have to explain themselves. Saw one's whole reasoning laid out on a notice board."

**What player does:** Wonders what "new judges" changed → investigates Byzantine shift

### Skeptical Guard
> "I've heard things get documented now. Every decision, written down. Can't just disappear claims anymore."

**What player does:** Curious about the registry → discovers audit chain existence

---

## World Flavor (Lead to Outcomes Passively)

### Notice Board (not an NPC, just text)
> "Prime Council Session - Tuesday. Request for domain reset filed. Reasoning available upon request."

**What player does:** Sees "prime council" mentioned casually → later recognizes it in gameplay → connects dots

### Overheard Conversation
> "...said they needed a tutor council because the three traditions disagreed on what counts as proof..."

**What player does:** Hears "three traditions" → doesn't know what yet → remembers it when encountering political/religious claims

### Graffiti or Rumor
> "The architects can reset if they mess up bad enough. But they have to tell everybody."

**What player does:** Notes that mistakes can be corrected → later understands "1/0" domain reset concept naturally

### Quest Giver Flavor
> "I want this claim properly validated. Not just one person's opinion. Get it in front of the right people."

**What player does:** Learns that claims need "right people" → naturally asks "who decides that?" → validator system emerges

---

## Opposition Clues (Make Stakes Visible)

### NPC Fear
> "Better not claim that publicly. Opposition watches validator decisions. They'll try to discredit you."

**What player does:** Realizes opposition is watching → understands validator reputation matters → feels stakes

### Discovered Document
> "Counter-claim filed by [Opposition Name]. Disputed methodology. Awaiting security council review."

**What player does:** Sees opposition engaging through validator system itself → understands they can't block it, only contest it

### Overhead Worry
> "The validators caught another one of their planted false claims. Registry now shows it was invalid. Getting harder to hide things."

**What player does:** Understands opposition strategies are failing → validator system is actually working

## 11-Fold / Harmonic Hints (Mysterious)

### Cryptic NPC
> "When enough validators agree, something shifts. Hard to explain. Like they're thinking together somehow."

**What player does:** Hears "thinking together" but doesn't understand → later realizes validators phase-lock → aha moment

### Legend/Lore Fragment
> "The old stories said that groups of 11 could reach clarity no individual could. Maybe that's not just legend anymore."

**What player does:** Notes "11" casually → later sees 11-fold mentioned in validator councils → realizes it's not random

## Emotional Four-Fold Breadcrumbs (Subtle Rewards)

### NPC Compliment
> "You've got clarity about this. Not just knowing facts, but understanding *why* they matter. That's rare."

**What player does:** Hears "clarity" as positive trait → game rewards it → player starts noticing emotional four-fold patterns

### Validator Comment (observed, not directed at player)
> "I changed my mind about that claim. The submitter showed integrity I didn't see before. That changes how I weight evidence."

**What player does:** Sees "integrity" matters to validation → realizes emotional traits affect outcomes

---

## Countdown Breadcrumbs (August 6 Approach)

### Calendar/NPC Reference
> "The countdown keeps going. 97 days until everything changes. Or so they say."

**What player does:** Notices "97 days" → realizes something is happening → feels urgency without knowing why

### Whispered Rumor
> "Something big launches August 6th. No one's saying what, but validators are preparing for it."

**What player does:** Connects validator system to countdown → realizes stakes are larger than individual claims

## Domain Progression Hints

### Early Game (Atomic Physics)
> "Atomic claims are easiest to validate. Clean measurements. No opinion. Just facts."

**What player does:** Assumes all validation is like this → sets expectation for later domains

### Mid Game (Psionics Teased)
> "They're accepting weirder claims now. Psionics, consciousness effects. Measurements are harder but... possible."

**What player does:** Wonders how validators handle harder domains → discovers tutor councils naturally

### Late Game (Magic/Emotion Hinted)
> "The best validators aren't the skeptics anymore. They're the ones who've learned to measure meaning itself."

**What player does:** Realizes system evolves → magic/emotion validation becomes plausible → sees emotional four-fold matters

---

## Power Armor Breadcrumbs (Undefined)

### Cryptic Clue
> "There's something about the way you move. Different. Like you're carrying weight that doesn't show."

**What player does:** Wonders what NPC means → realizes they have the armor → never fully explained, discovered through play

### Another NPC
> "That upgrade doesn't work like others. The more you understand the system, the more it does."

**What player does:** Connects understanding to power → realizes armor responds to learning → meaning emerges

---

## Implementation Notes

- **Never frontload the system.** Breadcrumbs should appear after player has made a few claims.
- **Let players ask NPCs directly.** "What's a validator?" should get answers, but phrased as world flavor, not tutorial.
- **Repetition with variation.** Same concept (prime councils, registry, validators) mentioned multiple ways in different contexts.
- **Opposition should use breadcrumbs too.** Opposition NPCs can reference validator system fearfully, teaching players it works.
- **World changes with time.** More breadcrumbs appear as countdown approaches August 6.
