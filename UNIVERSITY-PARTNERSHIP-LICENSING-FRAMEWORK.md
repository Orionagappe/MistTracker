**Document Type**: Strategic Partnership Agreement & Licensing Framework  
**Status**: Pre-Negotiation Blueprint  
**Date**: April 20, 2026  
**Classification**: Confidential - Partnership  
**Purpose**: To establish generous licensing terms for university partners, enabling them to generate revenue from client front-end while protecting core methodology and IP

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Mission**: Grant university partners commercial rights to the client front-end interface while maintaining Orion Digital Insights' ownership of core methodology, ensuring partners can monetize their involvement in MistTracker development.

**Core Principle**: University partners should benefit financially from their contributions to validation, development, and credibility. Revenue-sharing partnerships create aligned incentives and sustainable relationships.

**Key Terms**:
- **What Partners Get**: Exclusive/non-exclusive rights to develop, modify, and commercialize client front-end
- **What Orion Retains**: Core methodology, backend validation engine, patent IP, trade secrets
- **Revenue Model**: Tiered licensing (free for academic use, fee-based for commercial deployment)
- **Financial Benefit**: Partners receive licensing revenue, royalties, or profit-sharing as negotiated

**Expected Outcomes**:
- ✓ University partners generate meaningful revenue from participation
- ✓ Orion retains core competitive advantages
- ✓ Client front-end innovation accelerated through academic development
- ✓ Market penetration expanded (multiple front-end implementations)
- ✓ Partnership relationships strengthened through mutual benefit

## 3. LICENSING STRUCTURE

### 3.1 License Model

**Type**: Non-exclusive license grant (partners can develop their own, Orion can license to others)

**Scope**: 
- **Academic Use**: Free license for educational, non-commercial use
- **Commercial Deployment**: Fee-based licensing with revenue-sharing

**Duration**: 
- Initial term: 5 years (2026-2031)
- Renewal: 2-year automatic renewals with modification right
- Termination: With cause, 90-day notice; without cause, subject to wind-down provisions

**Territory**: Worldwide (subject to regulatory restrictions)

### 3.2 Academic Use License (Free)

**Eligibility**:
- University researchers using for educational/research purposes
- Non-commercial trials and pilots
- Internal institutional use
- Published research (with attribution)

**Terms**:
- No license fee required
- Attribution required ("Built with MistTracker, Orion Digital Insights")
- Must cite original university partnership and trial work
- Results/learnings shared with Orion (publication cooperation)
- Security and data handling per Orion's standards

**Usage Restrictions**:
- Cannot resell or sublicense
- Cannot integrate into commercial products without commercial license
- Cannot distribute to non-academic entities
- Cannot use for revenue-generating consulting

### 3.3 Commercial License

#### **Structure Options**

**Option A: Royalty-Based License**
- **Upfront License Fee**: $10,000-50,000 (depending on deployment size)
- **Royalties**: 10-20% of gross revenue from client front-end deployment
- **Minimum Annual Royalty**: $5,000 (after year 1)
- **Payment**: Quarterly, within 30 days of quarter end
- **Audit Rights**: Orion may audit partner books annually

**Option B: Fixed-Fee License**
- **Annual License Fee**: $20,000-100,000 (depending on deployment scale)
- **Covers**: All royalties, unlimited deployment
- **Price Tiers**:
  - Tier 1 (< $500K client revenue): $20,000/year
  - Tier 2 ($500K - $2M client revenue): $50,000/year
  - Tier 3 (> $2M client revenue): $100,000/year
- **True-Up**: Annual reconciliation based on actual revenue

**Option C: Profit-Sharing**
- **Upfront**: Reduced license fee ($5,000-15,000)
- **Profit Share**: 50% of net profit from front-end deployment
- **Shared Expenses**: Development, hosting, support costs split
- **No Minimum**: Only pay on actual profitability
- **Requires**: Financial transparency and monthly reporting

**Recommended Model**: Option A (Royalty-Based)
- Aligns incentives (partners benefit from client success)
- Scales with actual deployment
- Provides predictable minimum royalty
- Simple to audit and enforce

#### **Additional License Terms**

**Development Rights**:
- Partners may modify and extend front-end for deployment context
- Partners may create derivative works (improved UI, new visualization types)
- Derivative works owned by partner (not Orion)
- Partners can commercialize derivatives without additional royalty

**Sublicensing**:
- Non-exclusive sublicensing permitted
- Partners may license to consultants/system integrators
- Partner remains liable for sublicensee compliance
- 20% of sublicense royalties paid to Orion (pass-through)

**Updates and Improvements**:
- Orion provides quarterly updates to client front-end
- Partners automatically receive updates (no additional fee)
- Partners can choose to apply updates or maintain legacy version
- Orion provides 12-month support for each major version

**Support and Maintenance**:
- Orion provides technical support (email, documentation, quarterly calls)
- Partners responsible for deployment and end-user support
- SLA: Critical issues resolved within 48 hours
- Partners can request priority support (+$5K/year)

## 4. PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT FRAMEWORK

### 4.1 Standard Agreement Provisions

#### **Representations and Warranties**
- Orion represents it owns/controls the IP being licensed
- Partners represent they have authority to enter agreement
- Mutual warranty: will comply with applicable laws
- NO warranty of merchantability or fitness (as-is license)

#### **Intellectual Property**
- **Ownership**: Orion owns core IP, partners own modifications
- **License Grant**: Non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-bearing
- **Sublicensing**: Permitted with revenue-sharing
- **Improvement Feedback**: Partners share improvements with Orion (optional)
- **Cross-License**: Orion may use partner improvements for other licensees

#### **Confidentiality**
- Partners maintain confidentiality of trade secrets and backend implementation details
- Both parties protect each other's confidential information
- Exceptions: Legally required disclosure, court orders
- Duration: 5 years post-termination

#### **Indemnification**
- **Partner Indemnity**: Against claims partners' front-end implementation infringes third-party IP
- **Orion Indemnity**: Against claims core backend/IP licensing infringes third-party IP
- **Limitations**: No indemnity for partner modifications or sublicensee use

#### **Limitation of Liability**
- Neither party liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages
- Total liability capped at 12 months of license fees paid
- Exceptions: IP indemnification, breach of confidentiality

#### **Termination & Wind-Down**
- **For Cause**: Material breach with 30-day cure period
- **Convenience**: Either party, 90-day notice
- **Wind-Down**: 90-day transition period to wind down commercial deployment
- **Data**: Partners responsible for customer notification and data handling
- **Survival**: Confidentiality, indemnification, IP clauses survive termination

### 4.2 Partnership Agreement Variations

#### **Tier 1: Large Research University** (Priority Partners)

**Terms**:
- Exclusive front-end license (only this partner for their institution type)
