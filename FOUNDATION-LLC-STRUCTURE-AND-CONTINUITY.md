## 7. DECOHERENCE EVENTS: CONTINUITY BOUNDARIES

**Definition**: A Decoherence Event is a condition that renders continued project operation infeasible or inadvisable.

### 7.1 Hard Decoherence (Project Termination)

**Condition: Fatal Technical Failure**
- Core methodology proves unsound (validation fails despite efforts)
- Emergence theory does not compress context as claimed
- AI systems hallucinate despite observational grounding
- Patent invalidated or prior art discovered

**Response**:
- Conduct root-cause analysis
- Document lessons learned
- If appropriate: License IP to other parties
- If not: Archive documentation, dissolve LLC

**Probability**: Low (~5%) — Phases 1-9 already validate soundness

---

**Condition: Market Becomes Infeasible**
- Patent rejected with no grounds for appeal
- Competing patents make commercialization impossible
- Market demand does not materialize (< $50K revenue year 1)
- Three consecutive failed commercialization attempts

**Response**:
- Pivot to alternative revenue model OR
- Open-source the methodology (preserve academic legacy)
- Archive IP for future exploitation

**Probability**: Low-Medium (~10-20%)

---

**Condition: Founder Incapacity**
- Founder unable to continue (health, personal, etc.)
- No succession plan in place
- Project lacks independent team to continue

**Response**:
- Activate succession plan (Section 7.3)
- If succession fails: License to capable third party

**Probability**: Managed by succession planning

---

### 7.2 Soft Decoherence (Project Hibernation)

**Condition: Revenue Plateau at Minimal Levels**
- Revenue < $20K annually for 2+ consecutive years
- Cannot support even part-time maintenance
- Market shows no growth signal

**Response**:
- Move to "maintenance mode" (minimal cost)
- Archive active development
- Preserve IP and documentation
- Maintain patent maintenance (fees)
- Resume development if market signals improve

**Probability**: Medium (~30-40%) — Most likely temporary condition

---

**Condition: Technology Disruption**
- New AI architecture makes MistTracker methodology obsolete
- Different approach to AI knowledge representation becomes dominant
- Conventional AI systems cease to be primary AI architecture

**Response**:
- Assess applicability to new architecture
- If applicable: Adapt methodology
- If not: Preserve as historical archive, seek new application domain

**Probability**: Medium-High (long-term) — Accept as 5-10 year risk

---

### 7.3 Continuation Guarantees

**Measures to Prevent Decoherence**:

1. **Documentation Preservation**
   - All materials stored redundantly (local + cloud)
   - Access instructions maintained in secure location
   - GitHub/public repo for non-sensitive materials

2. **Succession Planning** (if applicable)
   - Identify successor (if applicable)
   - Document all processes and knowledge
   - Transition access and authority before incapacity

3. **Patent Maintenance**
   - Annual maintenance fees budgeted
   - Renewal deadline calendar maintained
   - Automatic payment setup (if possible)

4. **IP Insurance** (future)
   - Consider IP liability insurance
   - Patent defense fund (if patent disputes arise)

5. **Community Preservation** (if open-sourced)
   - Open-source license (e.g., Apache 2.0, MIT)
   - Community governance model
   - Maintenance via community contributions
