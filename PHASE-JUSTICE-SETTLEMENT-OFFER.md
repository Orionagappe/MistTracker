## 1. Settlement Offer Summary

This document outlines a formal settlement offer to resolve the ongoing dispute between [Developer/Defendant Name] and the class of customers regarding [Game Project Name]. The offer is made in an effort to reach resolution without further litigation or arbitration proceedings.

### Core Offer

[Developer/Defendant] agrees to release all documentation materials related to the game project under a Creative Commons license (specific license terms to be mutually agreed upon), making such materials publicly available under open licensing terms.

### Consideration

Upon acceptance of this offer and completion of the material release:
- All pending arbitration proceedings shall be suspended
- Parties may negotiate final settlement terms with professional mediation
- This offer may serve as evidence of good faith resolution efforts

---

## 2. Definition of Scope

### Documentation Materials Included

"Documentation materials" are defined as:
- Design documents and specification files
- Architecture and technical documentation
- Planning documents and roadmaps
- Asset inventories and resources lists
- Development notes and progress records
- Any written materials related to the game project

### Materials Explicitly Excluded (if any)

- Third-party proprietary code or assets
- Licensed intellectual property not owned by [Developer]
- Financial records or confidential business information
- Personal information of team members (to be redacted)
- Materials that would violate existing licenses or agreements

---

## 3. Creative Commons License Selection

The parties agree that materials shall be released under one of the following Creative Commons licenses:

**Option A: CC BY-SA 4.0 (Attribution-ShareAlike)**
- Most permissive for derivative works
- Requires attribution
- Derivative works must use same license

**Option B: CC BY 4.0 (Attribution Only)**
- Permissive for any use
- Requires attribution only
- Allows commercial use and modifications

**Option C: CC BY-NC 4.0 (Attribution-NonCommercial)**
- Non-commercial use only
- Requires attribution
- Allows modifications for non-commercial purposes

**Selection Process:**
- Plaintiff class nominates preferred license
- [Developer] has 5 business days to accept or propose alternative
- If no agreement, mediator selects CC BY 4.0 as default

---

## 4. Release Protocol & Timeline

### Phase 1: Acceptance (Days 1-7)
- This offer is presented to all parties
- Both [Developer] and plaintiff class representatives have 7 calendar days to accept terms
- Acceptance must be in writing by authorized representatives

### Phase 2: Material Preparation (Days 8-30)
- [Developer] assembles all documentation materials
- Materials are reviewed for scope compliance (excluded items removed/redacted)
- Legal review confirms no violation of third-party IP rights
- Plaintiff class has right to observe/participate in review process

### Phase 3: Release & Verification (Days 31-45)
- Materials are uploaded to public repository with Creative Commons license
- Release URL provided to all parties
- Independent verification that license is correctly applied
- Public notice issued (optional, parties decide)

### Phase 4: Confirmation (Day 46+)
- Upon verified completion, arbitration suspension takes effect
- Settlement terms documented
- Parties proceed to final mediation/negotiation if needed

---

## 5. Legal Framework

### Non-Admission of Liability

This offer is made without admission, guilt, or acknowledgment of wrongdoing by any party. The release of materials is a settlement consideration only and shall not be construed as:
- Admission of breach of contract
- Admission of fraud or misrepresentation
- Admission of failure to deliver
- Waiver of any defenses or counterclaims

### Conditions

This offer is contingent upon:
1. All parties agreeing to proceed with professional mediation/arbitration for final resolution
2. All parties maintaining confidentiality of settlement negotiation discussions (except as legally required)
3. [Developer] having clear rights to release the documentation materials
4. No revelation of trade secrets or proprietary information in released materials
5. Compliance with all applicable laws and licensing agreements

### Failure to Comply

If either party fails to comply with terms of this agreement after acceptance:
- Non-compliant party is in material breach
- All suspensions of arbitration are lifted
- Previous arbitration proceedings resume immediately
- Breaching party may be liable for legal costs and sanctions

## 9. Dispute Resolution if Terms Are Unclear

## 10. Termination & Withdrawal

### Withdrawal by Either Party

Either party may withdraw this offer by written notice if:
- No acceptance is received within 7 calendar days of presentation
- The other party materially breaches pre-acceptance obligations
- Court order prohibits proceeding

### Effect of Withdrawal

If offer is withdrawn before acceptance:
- No party admits any wrongdoing
- Settlement negotiations cease
- Arbitration/litigation resumes from previous status
- This document may not be used as evidence of liability or bad faith
